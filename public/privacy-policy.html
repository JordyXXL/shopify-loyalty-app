Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how LoyaltyEngage (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our app, which connects your webshop to a loyalty reward system.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data when our app is installed and used:

  • Store Information: Store name, email, domain, and plan details.
  • Customer Data: Names, emails, and purchase history only as needed to determine loyalty eligibility.
  • Cart & Order Data: Details of items in carts and completed orders, for the purpose of applying loyalty rewards.
  • App Usage Data: Usage patterns to improve app functionality and customer experience.

We do not collect or store payment information or personal data beyond what is necessary to provide our loyalty features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Automatically add discounted or free products to customer carts based on reward eligibility.
  • Track loyalty point redemptions and purchase behaviors.
  • Enable custom rule settings for loyalty programs.
  • Improve and personalize the app’s performance.
  • Provide support and app updates.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

We may share your information only with:

  • Shopify, for app integration and store functionality.
  • Authorized service providers, solely for app functionality (e.g., hosting, analytics).

All data sharing is governed by confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as your store uses the app. Upon uninstallation, your data is securely deleted from our servers within 30 days.

5. Data Security

We implement standard security practices to protect data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, including:

  • HTTPS encryption
  • Role‑based access controls
  • Secure server storage

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to data we’ve collected.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the app.
